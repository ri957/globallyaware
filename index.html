1. Reduce

Reduce means using less and in this case, creating less waste.

  • Turn off lights when not in use
  • Use less plastic or avoid it when possible
  • Only buy necessities

2. Reuse

Reuse means using things repeatedly instead of throwing them away after 1 or 2 uses.

  • Use reusable shopping bags
  • Refill water bottles
  • Donate old clothes

3. Recycle

Recycle means turning old materials to make new products.

  • Recycle paper and cardboard
  • Recycle glass bottles
  • Sort rubbish into the correct bins
